Quality and Certifications

How we check product quality and the standards we work to.

Quality assurance

At Nirdhara Overseas Pvt. Ltd., we are committed to supplying dehydrated food ingredients and spices that meet agreed customer specifications and applicable export requirements.

  • Product Specification Verification
    • Reviewing product details such as appearance, colour, moisture, form and packaging against buyer requirements.
  • Pre-Dispatch Quality Review
    • Coordinating final product and packaging checks before shipment to ensure conformity with agreed order specifications.
  • Export Documentation & Traceability
    • Maintaining relevant supplier, product and shipment records to support order identification and export documentation.

Certifications

Compliance

Nirdhara Overseas is committed to meeting applicable food safety, quality and export requirements for its products. Our compliance practices focus on regulatory adherence, product quality, proper documentation and destination-specific requirements.

Food Safety & Regulatory Compliance

We operate in accordance with applicable Indian food safety and export regulations, including relevant FSSAI requirements and applicable export registrations.

Product Labelling & Packaging

Product labelling, packaging specifications and documentation are aligned with applicable regulatory requirements and the requirements agreed with international buyers.

Destination-Country Requirements

We consider importing-country requirements relating to food safety, permitted residue limits, contaminants, microbiological parameters and product specifications before export.

Testing & Documentation

Relevant product test reports, certificates of analysis (COA), product specifications and supporting export documentation can be shared upon request, subject to product and shipment requirements.
